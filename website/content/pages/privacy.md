_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Malt Biscuit is a DJ and popup sound system based in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

## What we collect

This website doesn't use accounts, forms, analytics or advertising trackers. We don't collect personal information just because you visit.

If you contact us, for example to book a popup or ask a question through Instagram or email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and event information.

## How we use it

We only use the information you send us to reply to you, plan and run your booking, and keep basic records of the events we play. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, except where we need to in order to run your event (for example, with a venue) or where the law requires it.

## Third-party services

This site loads a web font from CDNFonts, which means your browser connects to their servers. Links to Instagram, our sponsors and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Get in touch using the details below.
