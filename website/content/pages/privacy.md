_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Malt Biscuit is a melodic house DJ and popup sound system based in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the [Privacy Act 2020](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2020/0031/latest/LMS23223.html) and its Information Privacy Principles.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Malt Biscuit.

## What we collect

We only collect personal information we need.

**When you send a booking enquiry**, we collect your name, email address, event date, whether you'd like the Malt Biscuit rig, and the details you write in your message (for example, the location and expected number of guests).

**When you send a message through the contact form**, we collect your name, email address and your message.

**When you contact us another way**, such as by phone or on Instagram, we receive whatever details you choose to share.

**When you book**, we may also collect what we need to run your event and get paid, such as a billing name and address, and details about the venue and the people we'll be working with on the day.

**When you visit the site**, our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies, and we don't build profiles of visitors.

## Why we collect it

We use your information to:

- reply to your enquiry or message
- prepare quotes, and plan, confirm and run your booking
- invoice you and keep business and tax records
- keep the site secure and working

We won't use your information for marketing, and we never sell it.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only when it's needed for the purposes above:

- **People involved in your event**, such as the venue or event organiser, when that's needed to run it.
- **Service providers** that help us run this site and our business (listed below). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
- **When the law requires it**, or to prevent a serious threat to someone's health or safety.

## Service providers, and storage outside New Zealand

Some of the services we use are based overseas, so your information may be stored or processed outside New Zealand:

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website and its forms (global network, United States based).
- **Resend** delivers form submissions to our email inbox (United States).
- **SoundCloud** plays our music on the home page. It only loads if you flip the record at the top of the home page. Your browser then connects to SoundCloud, which may set its own cookies (Germany and United States).

We choose providers that protect personal information with safeguards comparable to New Zealand's, as the Privacy Act requires when information is sent overseas.

Links to Instagram, our sponsors and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

## How we keep it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only Malt Biscuit can access our inbox and booking records.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries and messages that don't lead to a booking: up to 2 years, then deleted.
- Booking and invoicing records: 7 years, as required for New Zealand tax purposes.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it if it's wrong. You can also ask us to delete information we no longer need.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We'll respond as soon as we can, and within 20 working days. We may need to confirm your identity first. There's no charge for a request.

## Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz) or on 0800 803 909.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
